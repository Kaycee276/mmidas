## 💡 Executive Summary

Think of the shops you use every week on campus: your favourite food spot, the thesis printing lab, the hostel salon, or the tech hub repair shop. **What if you could own a piece of their success?**

**Midas** is a three-sided campus micro-investment and marketplace platform that connects university students with verified local businesses. By introducing transparent financial infrastructure, standardized reporting, and compliant profit-sharing, Midas turns everyday campus spending into student wealth creation and debt-free local business growth.

---

## ⚠️ The Problem: Capital Doesn’t Reach Campus Businesses

Campus economies across Nigeria run on essential local ventures—cafes, eateries, printing hubs, salons, and laundry centers—yet growth capital rarely reaches them:

* **For Merchants:**
  * Commercial banks and traditional venture capital view campus MSMEs as too small, informal, or high-risk.
  * Merchants lack structured, investor-ready tools to showcase their performance or report verifiable traction.
* **For Students:**
  * Students already know which campus spots have consistent queues and product-market fit.
  * However, they have had no accessible mechanism to discover legitimate opportunities, verify business legitimacy, understand clear investment terms, or track returns on their money.

> *"The demand and the trust are already there. The infrastructure isn't."*

---

## ✨ The Solution

Midas provides the missing financial and discovery infrastructure for university ecosystems:

1. **Track Every Naira:** Complete, transparent visibility across funding, wallet deposits, revenue reports, and payouts.
2. **Campus Marketplace & Map Discovery:** Location-aware campus discovery focusing on real student hubs (pioneered at the **University of Nigeria, Nsukka — UNN**).
3. **Micro-Equity Ownership:** Enables students to start building an investment portfolio from as low as **₦1,000**.
4. **Empowering Communities:** Converts loyal everyday customers into aligned stakeholders who champion the businesses they back.

## 🔄 How Midas Works (End-to-End Workflow)

Trust is built into every step of the workflow rather than added as an afterthought:

```
  ┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────────┐
  │  1. VERIFY   │ ──> │  2. INVEST   │ ──> │  3. REPORT   │ ──> │4. DISTRIBUTE │
  └──────────────┘     └──────────────┘     └──────────────┘     └──────────────┘
  Merchants register    Students discover    Merchants submit     Approved profit
  & upload KYC IDs,     businesses on the    revenue & expense    is automatically
  CAC docs & store      campus map, fund     reports; audited     split: 65% store,
  photos for audit.     wallets & invest.    by compliance team.  30% investors, 5% Midas.
```

1. **Verify (Onboarding & KYC):**
   * Merchants complete a streamlined KYC process: student/national ID, business registration (CAC/tax), proof of operating address, and storefront photos.
   * Compliance administrators inspect, approve, reject, or request resubmission through a dedicated audit log.
2. **Invest (Discovery & Micro-Equity):**
   * Students browse the campus marketplace via interactive Map and List views.
   * Students fund their wallets securely and purchase shares starting from ₦1,000.
3. **Report (Audited Financials):**
   * Merchants log periodic gross revenue, operating expenses, and net profit.
   * Admins audit and verify submitted reports against business metrics.
4. **Distribute (Shared Profit Payouts):**
   * Once approved, net profits trigger a transparent allocation workflow directly into merchant and student wallets.

## 👥 Built for Three Distinct Sides

Midas does not squeeze one generic interface into three personas. Each stakeholder has a tailored, purpose-built portal:

### 🎓 1. For Students (Investors)
* **Low Entry Barrier:** Invest from just **₦1,000**, democratizing wealth creation for all undergraduates.
* **Location-Based Campus Map:** Discover nearby shops with real-time walking distances (meters & km) using client-side geolocation.
* **Portfolio & ROI Analytics:** Track real-time portfolio valuation, total dividends received, capital growth, and transaction histories.
* **Integrated Wallet:** Deposit securely via Bachs checkout, hold balances, and withdraw eligible returns directly to bank accounts.

### 🏢 2. For Merchants (Business Owners)
* **Professional Storefront:** Showcase products, menus, physical storefront addresses, and verified campus landmarks (e.g., SUB Arcade, Franco Complex, Library Promenade).
* **Location & Map Pinning:** Drop and adjust physical shop pins on the interactive campus map for seamless customer foot-traffic.
* **Investor Dashboard:** Monitor total backers, debt-free capital raised, and backer engagement.
* **Financial Hygiene:** Builds consistent accounting and reporting habits through periodic revenue filing.

### 🛡️ 3. For Compliance Admins (Auditors)
* **KYC Review Queue:** Inspect identity documents, CAC papers, and physical shop photos.
* **Financial Audit Suite:** Review, verify, approve, or reject merchant revenue statements.
* **Audit Trail & Integrity:** Complete immutable audit history protecting investors, merchants, and the platform.

## 📈 The Business Model: Aligned Shared Profit

Midas operates on a sustainable shared-growth model. Midas **only earns when merchants are profitable**, guaranteeing complete alignment of incentives:

```
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │              APPROVED NET PROFIT BREAKDOWN             │
  ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │   🏪 65% — Merchant Retained Profit                    │
  │   🎓 30% — Student Investor Dividend Pool              │
  │   ⚡  5% — Midas Platform Sustainability & Compliance  │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Proportional Dividends:** Students share the 30% investor pool proportionally based on their share of active equity held in that venture.
* **Annual Payouts:** Distributions occur on scheduled cycles (e.g., annual January 1st distribution), backed by audited revenue reports.
* **Honest Transparency:** Returns depend on underlying merchant business performance and agreed terms; equity investments are not risk-free bank deposits. Emphasizing this honesty builds institutional credibility.
